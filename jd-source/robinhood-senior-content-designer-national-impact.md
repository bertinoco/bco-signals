Senior Content Designer, National Impact
Menlo Park, CA; New York, NY
Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you're ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

About the team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

In 2026, our team built an entirely new category of financial product in partnership with the U.S. Treasury —  investment accounts for every child born in America. The app debuted at #3 in the App Store and #1 in Finance. Now we're designing what comes next: new platforms and new experiences that will shape how future generations of Americans start building wealth. Getting every child invested early is how we continue to democratize finance for all.

As Senior Content Designer, you'll shape work from early concept through national launch, helping define the design direction for one of Robinhood's most visible product lines. Partnering with senior leadership, engineering, legal, research, data science, and marketing, you'll turn complex requirements into experiences that feel simple and trustworthy.

This role is for a content designer at the top of their craft who wants to make an impact on a national scale. Success looks like a generation of Americans entering adulthood already invested and confident in how money grows.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA and New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you'll do

Craft and refine UI content across new product experiences
Own decisions that impact terminology, content patterns, and design interactions
Build and maintain scalable systems that support large-scale initiatives
Influence product and design direction at the leadership level, including presenting to executives and external stakeholders, while keeping to tight timelines
Raise the bar for the broader content and design community through mentorship, reviews, or setting standards
Use AI tools to prototype, pressure-test content patterns, and speed up iteration, while applying your own judgment to keep quality high
Present rationale for decisions clearly and confidently
What you bring

Strong experience in content design, with a track record of building content systems and keeping terminology consistent at scale
Experience leading content strategy for a complex, high-visibility product
A portfolio that shows your craft and systems thinking 
Proficiency in Figma, and comfort working directly in design files alongside product designers
Excellent communication skills and the ability to partner with many stakeholders
Experience working in large, matrixed organizations, including presenting to leadership
Attention to detail in both your work and how you present it
Comfort with ambiguity and tight timelines, with the confidence to speak up and shape product direction
Willingness and curiosity to use AI in your content work
Nice to have: experience in finance or education-related products, or 0 to 1 work inside a larger organization
This position is restricted to US citizens or lawful permanent residents due to legal requirements.
What we offer

Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career
Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching
Top Tier benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents
Access to the Robinhood Employee Fund that gives eligible US employees the opportunity to invest in a private employee fund that provides exposure to Robinhood Ventures funds.
Access to the best AI tools on the market and continuous AI skill-building for every employee, technical or not.
Lifestyle wallet - a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more
Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits
Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more!
Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces.
