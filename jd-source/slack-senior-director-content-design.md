What You'll Be Doing & Why It Matters

Slack is at an inflection point. We're evolving from the world's most beloved team communication tool into something fundamentally new: an AI operating system where humans and agents work together to get things done. Built on the context of your work, Slack becomes a single, personalized command center that replaces fragmented apps, inboxes, and point solutions.

As agents take on more of the work, the words between humans and machines carry more weight than ever. They set expectations, signal intent, earn trust, and keep people in control. Content design is a key part of what makes Slack feel like Slack. As the platform evolves, content designers as language experts will be at the center of defining these new experiences. We're looking for a leader with deep UX and content craft, that is excited about defining the evolution of work and the judgment to work through the hard tradeoffs AI brings. You'll grow a high-performing team of content designers, champion their work and careers, and make content design a core part of how Slack builds its future.

How you'll lead:
You lead through coaching. You help designers make and own their decisions instead of speaking for them
You lead through advocacy. You open doors, add influence behind key decisions and protect your team's focus to help amplify their work.
You trust the team to set its areas of focus, and you build on the momentum that's already there
You look for growth opportunities for everyone on the team
You bring a growth mindset and real curiosity to a discipline that's changing fast


Core Responsibilities & Scope
Lead and grow a high-performing team of content designers who define how natural language, both written and spoken, shapes how people and agents work together in Slack. Champion your team's careers through performance, promotion, and growth conversations, and help them through people logistics like relocation and sabbaticals
Champion content design at the executive level, and own naming, terminology, and language decisions with product leadership. Partner with product design, product management, and engineering to make content design a core part of how Slack builds
Raise the bar on content craft and quality across AI-native experiences and traditional UI. Evolve team operations with the team, building on what works and creating clear paths for feedback to flow up
Lead and advocate for Help Center, including its content strategy, vendor partnerships, and cross-functional ownership


Minimum Requirements
Deep content design background with strong UX craft, and experience designing content systems that cut cross-platform confusion and keep language consistent across products, surfaces, and devices
Experience managing content design teams, ideally including Help or support content, with a track record of growing strong ICs and leaders. Experience leading distributed teams
Executive presence: you can explain the value of language systems clearly and influence product decisions at the senior level. Excellent track record of cross-functional collaboration
AI fluency grounded in judgment. You're excited about what AI makes possible and willing to engage with its ethical tradeoffs
