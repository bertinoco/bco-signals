Phase2 is seeking a Content Architect responsible for designing, governing, and operationalizing content architectures that support modern digital platforms, including AI-enabled experiences. This role ensures client content is structured, reusable, accessible, and trustworthy, enabling scalable delivery across websites, applications, personalization systems, and AI-driven interfaces.

By architecting content for both human users and machine learning systems, this role ensures clients capture opportunities across traditional search, generative engine optimization (GEO), and AI-powered experiences.

The Content Architect works hands-on with client teams, engineers, data specialists, and delivery leads to translate business and user needs into durable content models, taxonomies, and governance practices that can be implemented and sustained over time.

This position reports to: Director, Marketing Services

Compensation: Base annual salary of $120,000 - $142,000

What You'll Do

Lead the design of content models, taxonomies, and metadata structures that support modular, reusable, and semantically meaningful content.
Conduct content audits and analysis to inform platform migrations, redesigns, and AI-readiness efforts.
Lead structured data and schema strategy (e.g., Schema.org, provider/location/service entities), ensuring content is optimized for traditional SEO and generative AI discovery (GEO), including AI visibility requirements such as citability, entity clarity, and authority signals.
Own technical SEO considerations related to content architecture, including metadata strategy, structured data coverage, search visibility foundations, and semantic structure.
Define and implement content governance frameworks, including editorial workflows, lifecycle rules, and quality standards.
Ensure content architectures support accessibility (WCAG - Web Content Accessibility Guidelines), compliance, and consistency across channels and audiences.
Partner with Engineering and Data teams to ensure content structures are technically implementable within CMSs, APIs, and downstream systems.
Guide clients through content transformation and migration efforts, from strategy through execution, including designing content operations workflows to reduce content and metadata debt and improve system integrity.
Collaborate with Client Orchestrators to align scope, timelines, and delivery expectations.
Provide content architecture direction to delivery teams and mentor less senior content strategists.

What You'll Need

Deep experience in content architecture, taxonomy, and governance.
Hands-on delivery experience with CMS-driven platforms and migrations.
Strong collaboration with engineering and delivery teams.
Familiarity with AI-enabled content use cases (semantic search, personalization, automation).
Proven ability to lead complex content workstreams within client engagements.
Demonstrated experience diagnosing and remediating content quality issues at scale, including inconsistent tagging, taxonomy violations, metadata gaps, and redundant content structures.
Strong proficiency working in spreadsheets, structured datasets, system exports, and data normalization tools to support content cleanup, transformation, and migration workflows.
Strong working knowledge of technical SEO and structured data implementation, with experience using SEO platforms to diagnose and improve content visibility.
Experience defining structured data and schema strategies for SEO and AI visibility (e.g., Schema.org, JSON-LD, entity modeling).

What Success Looks Like

Content models that reduce duplication and rework.
Clean migrations and transformations with minimal content debt.
Platforms that support personalization, AI retrieval, and multimodal delivery without retrofitting.
Clients able to govern and evolve their content post-delivery.
Structured data implementations that improve SEO performance, GEO outcomes, and visibility within AI-generated answers.
