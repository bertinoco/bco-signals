Senior Information Designer
Charleston, SC
Who We Are: LINK is a fast-growing Woman Owned Small Business (WOSB) that leverages human-centered design to support strategy, innovation, communication, change, and branding within the federal government and adjacent industry partners. At LINK, we partner with engineers, futurists, and thought leaders to untangle complexity, discover opportunity, and communicate clearly with visual stories.

Let us be your partners in change.

Location: We are actively hiring in the Charleston, SC area.

About the Opportunity

LINK is actively seeking a Senior Information Designer to join our dynamic team, where we collaborate with clients across a wide range of industries to create impactful visual stories that communicate complex ideas. You will be responsible for translating concepts into compelling visual products, including prototypes, wireframes, infographics, charts, and diagrams, to effectively convey both quantitative data and abstract ideas in print and digital formats.

This is an exciting opportunity for a creative professional to apply human-centered design and design thinking principles to solve complex challenges and deliver visually engaging solutions. You will work closely with both internal teams and clients to understand their needs, develop intuitive information hierarchies, and create designs that support their goals.

Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communication, Visual Journalism, Animation, Information Design, or a related field.
10+ years of experience in graphic design, information design, visual communication, or a related field.
Current TS/SCI clearance level required.
Strong expertise in information design, visual storytelling, and communicating complex information through visual means.
Well-versed in a variety of design disciplines (e.g., information design, graphic design, brand development, experience design) and able to select the appropriate approach for a given challenge.
Strong understanding of information hierarchy, accessibility considerations, visual communication principles, and human-centered design methodologies.
Demonstrated ability to lead complex design efforts and translate information into clear, actionable visual products.
Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with clients and cross-functional teams.
Demonstrated ability to balance multiple priorities and deadlines while maintaining high standards of quality and craft.
Able to work independently while contributing effectively within collaborative team environments.
Capable of mentoring and coaching others.
Approaches work with optimism, openness to new ideas, and a collaborative mindset.
Expertise with Adobe Creative Cloud tools and the ability to guide design approaches, workflows, and tool selection that improve the quality, consistency, and effectiveness of visual products.
Portfolio that demonstrates both conceptual skills and design execution

Responsibilities

Strategic Visual Problem Solving
Serves as a trusted advisor to clients and project teams by translating complex challenges into clear communication strategies and visual approaches.
Partners with cross-functional teams, including strategists, design strategists, engineers, researchers, and subject matter experts, to understand organizational priorities, audience needs, and communication objectives.
Applies human-centered design principles to develop visual solutions that support stakeholder understanding, alignment, and decision-making.
Translates quantitative and qualitative data, research findings, and complex concepts into compelling visual narratives and communication products.
Develops information hierarchies, visual frameworks, and communication systems that improve comprehension, navigation, and usability.
Guides visual approaches that help stakeholders understand complex systems, processes, concepts, and strategic initiatives.

Visual Design & Information Products
Leads development of high-impact visual products including infographics, dashboards, diagrams, presentations, wireframes, prototypes, and decision-support materials.
Establishes illustration approaches, visual frameworks, icon systems, and storytelling techniques that strengthen communication and improve audience understanding.
Translates highly complex technical, operational, strategic, and research-driven information into compelling visual narratives and executive-level communication products.
Provides guidance on accessibility, inclusive design, and Section 508-compliant approaches that improve the usability and reach of visual products.
Establishes standards for templates, visual systems, and reusable assets that improve quality and consistency across engagements.
Ensures visual products reflect strong information hierarchy, visual clarity, accessibility, and audience-centered design principles.

Collaborative Design & Iteration
Collaborates effectively with internal and external stakeholders to develop visual solutions that address client objectives and audience needs.
Facilitates design discussions, reviews, and working sessions that strengthen project outcomes and build stakeholder alignment.
Uses visual prototyping techniques to explore concepts, communicate ideas, and evaluate potential solutions.
Synthesizes feedback from clients and teammates into actionable design improvements and strategic recommendations.
Balances multiple priorities while maintaining high standards of quality, consistency, and craft.

Brand & Creative Execution
Develops and applies visual identities, brand systems, and communication approaches across a variety of formats and audiences.
Provides guidance on typography, composition, hierarchy, color, illustration, and visual storytelling approaches.
Ensures visual products consistently align with audience needs, communication objectives, and established brand standards.
Demonstrates strong visual judgment and craftsmanship across all deliverables.
Brings creativity, curiosity, and a problem-solving mindset to complex communication challenges.

Interactive & Digital Media
Recommends the most effective medium or combination of media to communicate information, engage audiences, and support desired outcomes.
Guides development of digital, interactive, and multimedia experiences that improve audience understanding and engagement.
Applies storytelling principles across websites, dashboards, applications, motion graphics, video, and other communication channels.
Helps teams evaluate emerging technologies, platforms, and communication approaches that may improve audience reach and effectiveness.
Demonstrates curiosity and adaptability when exploring new communication mediums, technologies, and tools.

Team Development & Leadership
Mentors teammates through coaching, knowledge sharing, and constructive feedback.
Models LINK values and contributes to a collaborative, inclusive, and high-performing team culture.
Shares tools, methods, frameworks, and lessons learned that improve the quality and consistency of work across engagements.
Supports development of emerging practitioners through guidance on information design, storytelling, visual communication, accessibility, and design execution.
Contributes to the continued evolution of LINK's Information Design practice through innovation, experimentation, and continuous learning.
