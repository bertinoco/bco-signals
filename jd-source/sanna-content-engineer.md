Highlights
Why Sanna?

Sanna is building a new kind of accounting firm – designed from the ground up for a world where AI handles most of the manual work that has defined the industry for decades. We combine AI-driven workflows, modern technology, and deep professional expertise to deliver the peace of mind of a traditional accounting firm, while radically changing how the work is done behind the scenes.

We’re building Sanna with international scale in mind from day one. Starting with holding companies and expanding into operating companies, we’re creating a category-defining product in a domain where correctness, trust, and clarity are non-negotiable.

We’re a small, highly ambitious team building Sanna from Oslo with global reach. We value ownership, craft, and thoughtful speed – knowing when to move fast and when to be precise. Content design is not a service function here; it’s a core part of how decisions are made and how quality and clarity are upheld across product and company.
What we're looking for

We value judgment, taste, and learning ability over years of experience or specific tools.

Exceptional product sense and taste

You understand how language shapes trust, clarity, and behavior – especially in high-stakes domains like finance.

Strong UX and brand writing craft

You can simplify complex concepts into communication that feels calm, precise, and human – across product and marketing.

Systems thinker, not just a writer

You design frameworks, patterns, and structures that scale – not one-off pieces of copy.

AI-native and tooling-oriented

You’re excited about building agents, prompt libraries, and workflows that elevate quality – not just writing manually.

Enabler, not a bottleneck

You build systems, guidelines, and tools that empower the whole team to write well – without everything needing to go through you.

Relentless quality

You don’t settle for average. You aim for an excellent, cohesive experience across all touchpoints.

Exceptional written proficiency in Norwegian and English

You write fluently and precisely in both languages. You care deeply about nuance, tone, clarity, and consistency – and hold the same high standard across Norwegian and English.
