About the job
At Netflix, our mission is to entertain the world. Together, we are writing the next episode - pushing the boundaries of storytelling, global fandom and making the unimaginable a reality. We are a dream team obsessed with the uncomfortable excitement of discovering what happens when you merge creativity, intuition and cutting-edge technology. Come be a part of what’s next.

Join the team building the future of Netflix’s new entertainment offerings, including Livestreams, Podcasts, and shorter form entertainment. We want our members to enjoy Netflix regardless of the time of day, their mood, how much time they have available, or whatever topic that’s on their mind. If you’re a big picture thinker who understands how people love to engage with content around the world, we want you on our team!

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Leadership: identify business opportunities, articulate experience vision and elevate our global influence through compelling storytelling that drives cross-function alignment.
Collaborative Facilitation & Partnership: Lead collaborative work sessions with stakeholders at all levels, fostering cross-functional partnerships that define and execute this new area of business.
Problem-solving in Ambiguity: Dive into complex, undefined spaces to synthesize information, simplify problems and develop user-centered design approaches that reflect fresh perspectives
Experience Innovation: Innovate to meet new business and member needs while designing scalable solutions that work across Netflix’s global ecosystem
Member Advocacy: Champion the diverse needs of our global member base throughout your design process, ensuring that our new content experiences resonate across cultures and contexts

Skills, Characteristics & Qualifications

10+ years of experience designing user experiences for consumer-facing products
Exceptional communication skills, whether in small group discussions, large-scale presentations, or with executives
Demonstrated creative and analytical writing skills
Known for developing language frameworks and strategies for discovery and engagement across multiple UI platforms, such as TV, mobile and web 
Experience working on global products and partnering with Globalization
Comfortable moving between writing vivid copy, articulating the rationale behind word choices and drafting strategic internal documents.
Strong systems thinker, with proven ability to create or contribute to terminology systems and design systems and evolve them as business needs change
Experience collaborating with marketing and brand teams on promotion strategies/campaigns
Demonstrated success in driving 0-1 initiatives forward
Strategic thinker who can articulate a compelling product vision
Ability to dive into a space, gather context, synthesize, and make impact quickly
Demonstrates great judgement on asking the right questions and determining the work that needs to be done to push the business forward
Is a fierce advocate for the user, and has the courage to speak up and influence 
Skilled at connecting the dots across related initiatives and fostering cross-functional collaboration
Comfortable navigating ambiguity and distilling complex, unstructured opportunities into actionable roadmaps that integrate user, technology, and business needs
Proven ability to deal with requirements from various teams, while not losing sight of long-term experience goals
Proficiency working within design tools, such as Figma
Great at receiving and giving feedback
