About Pinterest:

Millions of people around the world come to our platform to find creative ideas, dream about new possibilities and plan for memories that will last a lifetime. At Pinterest, we're on a mission to bring everyone the inspiration to create a life they love, and that starts with the people behind the product.

Discover a career where you ignite innovation for millions, transform passion into growth opportunities, celebrate each other's unique experiences and embrace the flexibility to do your best work. Creating a career you love? It's Possible.

At Pinterest, AI isn't just a feature, it's a powerful partner that augments our creativity and amplifies our impact, and we're looking for candidates who are excited to be a part of that. To get a complete picture of your experience and abilities, we'll explore your foundational skills and how you collaborate with AI.

Through our interview process, what matters most is that you can always explain your approach, showing us not just what you know, but how you think. You can read more about our AI interview philosophy and how we use AI in our recruiting process here.

We're looking for an early-to-mid career Content Designer to join our Personalization team. You'll help design personalized experiences that help people discover ideas they'll love, while preserving the moments of serendipity that makes Pinterest unique. As a Content Designer II, you'll work on surfaces like Homefeed to help users understand, navigate, and trust experiences that adapt to their interests, behaviors, and needs.

Content Designers at Pinterest are advocates for the user experience. We use language, information hierarchy, and content strategy to shape product experiences. The ideal candidate is a thoughtful content designer who understands how content can influence the direction of an experience, transform complexity into clarity, and create meaningful experiences at scale.

What you'll do:

Partner with Product, Design, Engineering, Research, Data Science, and more to shape personalized experiences across Pinterest, including Homefeed and other discovery surfaces.

Apply content design, information design, and user-centered thinking to create and refine product experiences that help people discover ideas, understand recommendations, and engage with personalized experiences.

Leverage research, data, and user insights to inform content decisions and identify opportunities to improve the Pinterest experience.

Contribute to scalable content frameworks, patterns, and standards that help create clear, consistent, and trustworthy personalized experiences.

Help create transparent, user-centered experiences by using language to explain why people are seeing content and how they can shape their experience.

Explore and apply emerging AI tools and prompting approaches across the content design process (from discovery and concepting through evaluation and iteration) to improve decision quality, craft, and efficiency while applying strong content design judgment and user-centered thinking.

Contribute to the broader Content Design practice by sharing learnings, evolving team best practices, and strengthening how we approach personalization at Pinterest.

What we're looking for:

3+ years of experience in user-focused product content design, ideally with experience designing complex, data-informed, or personalized experiences.

A Content Designer who goes beyond the string to help shape the content approach for a feature or experience.

A collaborative partner who works effectively with cross-functional teams and brings a content design perspective grounded in user needs, clarity, and design principles.

A portfolio demonstrating experience solving product problems through language, content strategy, and collaboration.

A learner and builder mindset, with curiosity and willingness to explore emerging AI capabilities and apply them appropriately to content design workflows and product experiences.

Bachelor's degree in design, a related field or equivalent experience.
