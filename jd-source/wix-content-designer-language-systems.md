Job Description
As a Content Designer, Language & Systems, you'll join a core team within the Writers Guild, building the tooling, documentation, standards, and evaluation workflows that make it possible to generate and update UX content at scale, reliably and consistently, in line with our guidelines. This is a central role that shapes the content across our product that reaches users every day, and it calls for a systems thinker — someone who can see content problems clearly and build solutions that scale, working as a true team player across disciplines. You'll be central to defining how content governance works across both human and agentic workflows, while also educating writers and non-writers on these systems and best practices. You'll mentor UX writers and collaborate closely with infra teams, designers, product managers, and developers throughout the product lifecycle.

In your day-to-day, you will:
Define new strategies for improving the quality of generated UX content across Wix
Build and evolve content governance processes
Identify gaps and create scalable systems to address them
Mentor UX writers and Wix creators, fostering a culture of craft
Champion the adoption of AI tools by running workshops, sharing updates, identifying opportunities for adoption, and participating in guild content reviews
Collaborate with product localization, customer care, and marketing teams when tools can impact their day-to-day

***
At Wix, we believe our best work happens together. Our work model is fully in person, with 5 days a week from our office. Flexibility remains a core value at Wix and special requests are handled thoughtfully at the team level.
Requirements
3+ years of experience as a UX writer or Content Designer
Proven leadership or managerial experience in the field of UX writing, content design, or content strategy
Genuine enthusiasm for AI tools and integrating them into practice
You have built systems other teams depend on: experience creating programmatic style guides, evaluation frameworks, reusable UI patterns, or rule-based terminology systems that became the authoritative source of truth for an entire organization
Understanding of localization process and workflows: Experience building core frameworks that naturally scale across languages, regions, and assistive technology contexts
Exceptional communication skills in native level English — written and verbal
Experience in large organizations with an understanding of how to scale – an advantage
Hebrew proficiency – an advantage
