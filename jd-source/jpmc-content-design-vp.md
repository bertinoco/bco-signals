JOB DESCRIPTION

As a Vice President of Content Design in Co-Brand Card, you will develop and execute content plans for inclusive, usable product experiences and play a pivotal role in shaping customer experiences through content. Lead strategically important initiatives, drive innovative solutions to complex problems, and collaborate with cross-functional teams. Leverage your expertise in content design and content architecture and provide guidance and direction in the narrative structure of products and services. Create engaging, user-friendly content that effectively communicates complex concepts to a diverse audience. Champion inclusive design and customer experience and contribute to the success of JPMorgan Chase's user experience design efforts.
In this role, you will architect and deliver modular, scalable content systems for the Co-Brand portfolio—creating reusable frameworks, templates, and governance that scale across multiple partners and cards while tailoring variants to each partner’s brand, legal requirements, and customer needs. You will set strategy, lead execution, and measure impact in close partnership with Product, Partner Portfolio, Development, Data, and Research. Your success will depend on excellent content craft and strong leadership capabilities. You’ll work collaboratively with Research and Design to clarify customer needs and build a deep understanding of the business to drive outcomes.
Job Responsibilities
Develop and implement content design strategies for multiple product components, ensuring alignment with business and user experience goals
Facilitate end-to-end discovery workshops with designers to understand narrative and optimal content architecture
Use content models and taxonomies to design, structure, and organize digital content for intuitive customer experiences
Collaborate with cross-product teams to develop consistent voice and narrative structures
Champion inclusive language for a diverse audience, ensuring positive customer interactions
Design and lead content deliveries that meet legal requirements and accessibility standards
Lead legal and compliance reviews, designing accessible content that safeguards the business and advocates for customers
Architect scalable content systems with clear governance and versioning for partner-specific customization
Create partner playbooks and variation guidelines to preserve coherence while tailoring content to each partner’s needs
Drive delivery at scale: manage content pipelines and release plans across multiple partner programs
Coordinate cross-functional handoffs with Product, Engineering, Legal, and Marketing

5+ years of experience or equivalent expertise in content design, editing, writing, strategy, process, and architecture for digital products and platforms
Ability to design effective content architecture for enhanced user experience and easy navigation
Expertise in written and verbal storytelling, clarifying complex concepts for diverse audiences
Extensive experience facilitating workshops for product and experience design
Advanced technical literacy and deep understanding of content platforms and their impact on user experience
Champion inclusive, accessible language for a diverse audience
Experience building scalable content frameworks in multi-brand or co-brand environments
Background in content operations and familiarity with design systems/component libraries
Ability to translate partner agreements and regulatory requirements into actionable content standards
Proven track record balancing strategic planning with hands-on delivery across complex, cross-functional programs
