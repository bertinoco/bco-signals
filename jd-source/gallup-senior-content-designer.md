Senior Content Designer
San Francisco
Design experiences that transform Gallup's research into stories people understand, trust and act on.

As a senior content designer at Gallup, you'll shape how people experience our brand across our public website and digital products. Working alongside content strategists, product designers and researchers, you'll translate complex research into intuitive digital experiences through thoughtful content structure, sequencing and information design. Your work will help people confidently navigate Gallup's marketing experiences and digital products, empowering them to understand insights that improve workplaces and lives around the world.

What You'll Do

Design the narrative flow, information architecture and visual structure of marketing pages using Gallup's established design system and reusable components
Shape in-product content experiences through thoughtful navigation, labels, wayfinding and interface content that help users confidently accomplish their goals
Collaborate with content strategists, product managers, designers and researchers to translate complex research, analytics and scientific concepts into clear, accessible experiences
Create reusable content patterns and design approaches that strengthen consistency across products and marketing experiences
Use information design and data storytelling techniques to help users quickly understand insights and take meaningful action
Champion clarity, consistency and usability across every customer touchpoint while balancing user needs with Gallup's voice and design standards
What Makes You Stand Out

Editorial judgment: You instinctively know what information matters most, what to emphasize and how to simplify content to create the greatest impact.
Information architecture: You naturally structure content and navigation so people always know where they are, what comes next and why it matters.
Systems mindset: You create elegant experiences within established design systems while making every interaction feel intentional and intuitive.
Analytical storytelling: You transform research-heavy, technical and data-rich information into experiences that are clear, compelling and easy to understand.
Collaborative partnerships: You build trusted relationships across design, content strategy, product management and research, welcoming feedback and improving ideas together.
What You Need

Bachelor's or master's degree in design, English, journalism, communications, human-computer interaction or a related field, or equivalent experience, required
At least five years of professional experience designing content across web, product or editorial contexts required
Portfolio demonstrating your ability to structure, sequence and communicate content across digital experiences required
Professional proficiency with Figma required
Experience using AI to enhance content design required
Background in editorial design, brand publishing or product content design preferred
A commitment to working on-site at Gallup's San Francisco office at least three days per week required
Eligibility to work in the United States required; this position is not eligible for employment visa sponsorship
