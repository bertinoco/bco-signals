CONTENT DESIGNER
Location: Barcelona, Spain
Category: Other
As a Content Designer on Monopoly GO!, you'll create polished, player-focused narrative and creative content that brings our game experiences to life. You'll be responsible for theming features from concept through delivery, making thoughtful creative and craft contributions that support memorable, delightful player moments.

You'll work closely with partners across disciplines to understand product, seasonal, brand, and player goals. As an individual contributor, your primary role will be helping the team handle the day-to-day creative problem solving tasks.

What You'll Do
Execute on content plans for features, events, and seasonal experiences
Create design documents and pitches
Help assess content needs for features
Design theming and narrative for exciting content such sticker sets, minigames, character designs and more
Help maintain the consistency and integrity of the Monopoly brand, as well as any brands of our licensed IP partners
Apply knowledge of player empathy and knowledge of the game and brand to create content aimed to deliver delight, wonder, and meaningful player moments
Clearly communicate creative concepts, decisions, opportunities, and solutions through reviews, presentations, and supporting documentation
Participate actively and constructively in creative reviews and planning
Work with production to balance quality, timelines, dependencies, and production requirements
Develop a strong understanding of our team's creative and design goals in order to contribute ideas and solutions that advance those goals
Build productive working relationships with partners across disciplines, proactively seeking context and incorporating different perspectives
Stay aware of changes and priorities across the broader team and adapt your work appropriately as needs evolve
Demonstrate Scopely's tenets in day-to-day work, treating colleagues with fairness, respect, empathy, and compassion.

What We're Looking For
3+ years of relevant professional experience in content design, game design, narrative design, game writing, creative development, or a related discipline.
Creative problem solving skills.
Resourceful and strategic thinking.
A portfolio or body of work demonstrating creative or narrative content and strong craft fundamentals.
Experience with short-form writing skills, including either/or copywriting, UX Writing
Experience contributing to scoped creative work from concept through implementation and delivery.
Strong understanding of how narrative, creative content, and design choices contribute to the overall player experience.
Ability to work within limitations such as technical constraints, brand restrictions or game mechanics.
Strong player empathy, appeal, and an ability to create content with a clear understanding of the intended audience, brand, and player experience.
Ability to communicate complex creative ideas and rationale clearly creative stakeholders and external partners.
Ability to leverage multiple IPs into a satisfying, cohesive experience for players.
Strong collaboration skills and the ability to build productive relationships across disciplines.
Ability to receive, evaluate, and act on feedback while also providing thoughtful and constructive feedback to peers.
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, including consistent documentation, file management, and adherence to established production conventions.
Experience using tools such as JIRA, Google Suite, Slack, Miro, Zoom, Airtable, Localization databases
Strong interest in mobile games.
Strong knowledge of pop culture/cultural zeitgeist.
Willingness to attend occasional offsites and Scopely team-building events.

BONUS:
Previous experience as a creative in the entertainment industry, either in film, animation, storyboarding, live theater, theme park design, or escape room design
Previous experience in the visual arts
