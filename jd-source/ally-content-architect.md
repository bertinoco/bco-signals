The Opportunity
Ally is seeking a Content Architect to optimize a content ecosystem built for AI-first discovery. Acting as a connective engine between editorial, UX, data, PR and SEO/GEO teams, the Content Architect sits above channels and formats. The ideal candidate will blend systems thinking, editorial strategy, consumer journeys and data fluency with a hands-on, action-oriented mindset. They don’t just identify opportunities — they activate them, bringing structure, clarity, and progress to ensure Ally’s content is discoverable, understandable and surfaced effectively by both humans and machines. This role is built for a builder and connector: a person who moves quickly, aligns teams, removes friction and drives momentum across a content ecosystem designed for AI-first discovery.
This role is focused less on creating content and more on making content work, designing systems structures and standards that turns content into a high-performing business asset. By aligning cross-functional teams around shared content structures and standards, the Content Architect ensures Ally’s content functions as a cohesive system: improving performance in traditional search, generative engines and AI-powered discovery while reducing duplication and operational friction. This role will own an end-to-end strategy to capture demand earlier in the journey and guide consumers seamlessly from AI-driven entry points to Ally-owned experiences.
The Work Itself
Drive cross-functional alignment and execution — turning insights into action and standard processes
Identify strategic content opportunities using owned and third-party data, with a focus on AI-first discovery, search demand and emerging trends
Own and evolve Ally’s content architecture across channels and partners, ensuring content is structured, connected, reusable and easily discoverable
Define and govern content models, taxonomies, metadata and structured data standards that enable modular, machine-readable, high-performing content
Partner with UX, editorial and product teams to align content structure to priority consumer journeys (acquisition, servicing, support) and reduce friction from entry to conversion
Lead content audits and ecosystem analyses to identify structural gaps, duplication and opportunities for consolidation, reuse and personalization
Establish success metrics and measurement frameworks for content performance (discoverability, engagement, reuse, workflow efficiency, personalization outcomes, SEO/GEO impact) and translate insights into a prioritized roadmap
Partner with engineering teams to operationalize architecture-driven optimization (internal linking strategy, schema/structured data and other technical considerations).
Collaborate with analytics team to build and maintain dashboards and reports that monitor content ecosystem health and outcomes over time
Ensure content and experiences meet legal, compliance and accessibility requirements
The Skills You Bring
Minimum Qualifications
7+ years of relevant experience or equivalent combination of education and experience
High school Diploma or GED equivalent
Preferred Qualifications
Proven ability leading cross-functionally and managing stakeholders across UX, analytics, editorial, marketing, legal/compliance and technology — aligning teams
Excellent communication and facilitation skills; able to translate complex concepts into clear decisions, reports and presentations  
Track record of building and governing content models, including taxonomies, metadata standards and structured data/schema to enable modular, reusable machine-readable content
Strong knowledge of how content is discovered across traditional and AI-powered discovery (SEO fundamentals plus GEO concepts such as entity optimization, structured data, retrieval signals.)
Practical knowledge of structured content and machine-readable signals, including schema/structured data, entity optimization and API-based integrations
Hands-on familiarity with SEO and AI search performance tools (seoClarity, Scrunch, etc.); experience with taxonomy management, workflow platforms and content performance dashboards
Demonstrated learning agility and comfort operating in a fast-changing environment; able to adapt quickly and execute with ambiguity
