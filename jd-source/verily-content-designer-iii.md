Who We Are

Verily Health is a data platform and technology company purpose-built to power AI-enabled precision health solutions that accelerate research and improve care for individuals and communities. Uniquely positioned at the intersection of technology, data science, and healthcare, Verily transforms multimodal health data into insights, models, and actions that make healthcare more personalized, predictive, and precise.
Description
As a UX Content Designer on our Platform team, you will develop best practices, write UX copy, and develop AI agents for our suite of health data products. This role sits at the intersection of UX writing and content strategy, requiring expertise in systems thinking, and a user-centric mindset. If you love problem solving, geek out over terminology and IA, and love the idea of making complex products intuitive, then we are looking for you!
Responsibilities
Transform clinical and business jargon into simple, helpful and clear content for data providers, configurators, and/or biomedical researchers.
Develop frameworks, strategies, and best practices that champion the end-user's needs.
Identify and document requirements, best practices, and prompts for AI agents that support and simplify end-user tasks in valuable, intuitive, and ethical ways.
Collaborate with designers, researchers, data scientists, product managers, and engineers; influence and adapt to stakeholders and their feedback.
Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in English, Communications, Science Writing, UX, HCI, or a related field.
5+ years of experience in UX writing, content design, or content strategy.
An outstanding portfolio of thoughtfully designed content, demonstrating work across the end-to-end content development process, from identifying best practices to refining copy.
Preferred Qualifications
Past work that focuses on Enterprise products, Healthcare, Medical services, or data-intensive technical platforms..
Strong understanding of AI, machine learning, and data visualization principles.
Excellent communication, presentation, interpersonal, and collaboration skills; ability to communicate complex concepts effectively across a broad range of audiences, and galvanize different opinions around a shared objective.
Familiarity with Figma as well as the technical intricacies of designing accessible, responsive web applications.
