Brief info about Vinted 

Our mission is to make second-hand the first choice, and we're looking for people who want to help us get there. Every day, we work together to help our members buy and sell pre-loved clothing and lifestyle items, giving each piece a second life – or even a third.
The Vinted Group is made up of three business units that support this mission:

Vinted Marketplace is Europe's leading platform for second-hand fashion and a go-to destination for all kinds of pre-loved items, with a growing range of categories. Our platform connects millions of members across 20+ markets, helping great items find a new life.

Vinted Go enhances the shipping experience with a vast network of over 500,000 pick-up and drop-off points, partnering with more than 60 carriers across Europe, with added services like item verification for peace of mind on high-value pieces.

Vinted Pay is the newest part of the Vinted Group, dedicated to bringing secure, reliable payments to buyers and sellers across Europe. Seamlessly integrated into the Vinted app, it helps keep every transaction safe, efficient, and easy for our members.

Founded in 2008 in Lithuania, Vinted began as a way for friends to find new homes for clothes they no longer needed. In 2019, we became Lithuania's first unicorn! Today, our headquarters remain in Vilnius, and we've grown with offices across Europe, supported by a team of over 2,000 people. 

Information about the position 

Vinted's Supply domain drives the seller experience at Vinted, by developing easy listing experiences that produce high quality, searchable listings that sell fast. You'll be working within the team, a cross-functional group of taxonomists, decision scientists, and product managers. Our goal is to enhance Vinted's listing, browsing, search, and item retrieval. As a taxonomist, you will build the structural logic for navigation, search relevance, listing quality, and product expansion, ensuring a coherent, seamless, and relevant end-to-end category experience. These data models and structures help Vinted's buyers find unique items and grow Vinted's global business. You'd be joining our team at a critical time: we are adapting our data models and systems to better support Vinted's transformation into a multi-vertical marketplace. 

As part of taxonomy projects, you will research, design, implement, and evaluate ontology and taxonomy changes. Your goal will be to promote and balance the needs of sellers and buyers in our C2C marketplace, as well as machine learning models. You will work closely with internal teams to explore alternative ways of modelling our data.

This role is perfect for someone who has experience designing and implementing taxonomies and ontologies, especially in an eCommerce environment.

In this position, you'll

Design, revise, and maintain buyer and seller-facing product taxonomies and navigation, across a wide range of product verticals
Design, implement, and maintain ontologies and data structures that support our member-facing content
Balance Vinted's Member experience with taxonomy and metadata heuristics while maintaining Vinted's tone and voice.
Research and build product attributes to help buyers narrow their search, and sellers describe their items.
Work with user research and data analysts to build and test taxonomies and metadata structures for the most impactful results.
Document internal standards, workflows, governance processes, and data models.
Collaborate and connect with a wide range of internal teams including: engineers, product managers, designers, data scientists, legal, policy, communications, localisation, and customer support.
