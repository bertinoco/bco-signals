Senior Conversation Designer

What Your Experience Will Be
We're seeking an experienced Senior Conversation Designer to join our team building and iterating on our generative AI voice system. This role requires both a designer and builder mindset to advance our product, and you'll have the opportunity to craft exceptional conversational experiences that feel natural, intuitive, and helpful to our users, across both voice and chat.

Day to day, you're digging into conversation transcripts and metrics to find where real users are hitting friction, evaluating how models perform in production, and partnering closely with engineers to define prompts and guardrails for genAI. You're as comfortable creating a consistent voice, tone, and personality as you are debugging a fallback path or pushing prompt changes into Github.

You'll report to our Head of Product, and partner with the Lead Conversation Designer to apply and contribute to design standards and processes. Beyond design, you'll collaborate with product, engineering, and operations to align conversation design with broader product strategy. You'll also drive research and testing across a range of methodologies — user research, usability testing, A/B testing, QA, and regression testing — to keep improving the experience. The role is based in our NYC HQ, with occasional travel for company offsites.

(Note: Conversation Design is called different things at different companies; even if your past titles don't match, we're looking for someone who can design AI and its underlying logic and training.)

Why You Belong Here and How You Will Grow
Slang's conversation design practice pushes for highest-in-industry standards, valuing both user-centered thinking and technical fluency. Our conversation designers are embedded in the engineering team, hold vision for the overall product implementation, and advise engineers on what to build.

You'll fit in here if you can obsess about building the right logic, advise on agent memory and state-based best practices, and use data to drive decisions that improve people's experiences across interfaces.

You'll thrive here if you are enthusiastic about shaping how conversation design gets done at Slang — the standards, the patterns, the reusable building blocks other designers build on. You'll grow technically by going deeper into LLM integration and prompt engineering alongside engineers who take that work seriously, and you'll contribute to maturing design practice with real standards and processes behind it.

What Success Looks Like
You will...
Design end-to-end conversational flows for generative AI capabilities, for both voice and chat that incorporate condition logic, parameter handling, and tool calls
Build robust error handling and fallback mechanisms for conversation recovery
Create and test dialog that maintains a consistent voice, tone, and personality across the product
Partner with engineers to define prompts and guardrails for LLM integration, and evaluate LLM performance in production
Analyze conversation transcripts, audio, and metrics to identify friction points and turn them into concrete design improvements
Define and document conversation design standards, best practices, and reusable patterns that scale across experiences

What You Will Bring
Required:
7+ years of experience designing conversational interfaces (voice assistants, IVR systems, any kind of voice AI)
Demonstrated expertise with both traditional NLU frameworks and generative AI/LLM technologies
Experience contributing to design standards and following established processes
Experience with evaluating genAI agents and metrics-driven design
Proven ability to design and build technically complex flows with multiple logical branches
Experience with parameter extraction, validation logic, and dynamic response generation
Strong understanding of programming concepts like variables, loops, and conditional statements
Proficiency implementing conversation flows that integrate with external systems via APIs
Proficiency with conversation design tools and prototyping platforms
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with an ear for natural language
Active, hands-on use of AI tools in your daily workflow — not passive awareness, but a demonstrated habit of using AI to move faster and think better

Preferred:
Strong understanding of linguistics, conversation analysis, and pragmatics
Experience with evaluation and analytics tools for conversation performance measurement
Understanding of accessibility considerations in voice and text interface design
Experience in the restaurant or hospitality industry
Familiarity with prompt engineering for large language models
Background in content design, strategy, or UX/UI design
Fluency in Spanish or French

How Work Affects My Life
As the senior conversation designer on a team that takes this craft seriously, you'll own meaningful design work end-to-end with real impact on how thousands of interactions land.
