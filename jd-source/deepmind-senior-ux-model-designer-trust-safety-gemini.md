About the job

As a Model UX Designer focused on the quality and safety of the Gemini model's output, you will build new capabilities for users and help them get the best out of the product. You will work in a rapidly evolving, exciting area with a dedicated and passionate team.

In this role, you will be a systems thinker with strong skills in content strategy, UX design, and problem-solving, anchored by a passion for the craft of writing. You will have experience developing guidelines, exemplary content, evaluations, and rubrics for LLMs, as well as writing and editing in a fast-paced, agile environment. Responsibilities

Apply deep understanding of model capabilities to develop system instructions and prototyping strategies, ensuring the delivery of consistently high-quality, safe, and helpful model outputs.
Diagnoze content quality issues and Initiate qualitative assessments of model performance, applying mastery of the eval process to develop strategies for continuous quality hillclimbing to improve experiences across product areas.
Develop strategies for improving model response quality, write instructions, guidelines, and standards to guide the work of reviewers and engineers.
Partner with AI product designers to craft leading-edge multimodal solutions, ensuring model responses and UI components work in concert to deliver cohesive, high quality experiences.
