About the job
About The Ride Platform

The Ride Platform, Inc. is the parent company of Outdoorsy, Ride, Wheelbase, and Roamly. We are named for the single transaction where an operator meets a renter. We build end-to-end for the Operator Economy: the marketplaces to book (Outdoorsy, Ride), the enterprise SaaS to manage (Wheelbase), and the insurtech (Roamly) to protect the assets people turn into businesses. We enable millions of people to explore the world while helping independent operators build generational wealth. Our mission is to build the essential infrastructure for the mobility economy.

About The Role

We are redefining how content gets created, scaled, and activated across marketing. This role owns content strategy end-to-end and plays a hands-on role in content creation, while also designing and operating content as a system. The focus is on building structured, repeatable workflows that turn strategy into execution efficiently and predictably.

In a highly technical buying environment, this role is responsible for producing content directly, guiding content strategy across campaigns, and ensuring content moves through a well-managed content pipeline from conception to activation.

Jasper is core to how we work. This role makes it the foundation of our content systems and workflows.

What You Will Own


Content Strategy and System Design

Define and evolve our content strategy across the full customer journey in close partnership with Product Marketing and Demand Generation
Design content systems that translate strategy into structured inputs, templates, and repeatable outputs across the content pipeline
Personally contribute to content creation where appropriate, using AI to accelerate drafting, iteration, and scaling
Ensure content supports discoverability, engagement, and content pipeline impact, not just production volume


AI-Powered Content Systems

Make AI, primarily Jasper, a core engine for content creation and scale
Establish standards for AI-generated content including prompts, templates, tone, guardrails, and quality thresholds
Build and maintain AI-powered content systems that support predictable, high-quality output across campaigns, channels, and markets
Ensure content systems are designed for reuse, modularity, and efficiency across the content pipeline


Customer Content Systems and Case Study Evolution

Redefine the case study process end to end as a scalable customer content system
Design workflows that maximize the value of each customer engagement by generating multiple downstream outputs from a single source
Partner with Product Marketing, Demand Generation, PR, Events, and Digital teams to orchestrate customer-derived content such as case studies, video interviews, webinars, contributed articles, press opportunities, and campaign assets
Establish templates, interview frameworks, consent models, and activation plans to ensure customer content is reusable across the content pipeline


Content Enablement and Workflow Design

Design and document workflows that shorten the path from campaign conception to launch
Build templatized content formats such as campaign kits, landing pages, emails, nurture tracks, thought leadership assets, and customer content modules that can be reused and adapted
Enable and train marketers across the organization to leverage content systems, templates, and workflows independently


GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Build and own our GEO framework, ensuring content is optimized for AI-driven discovery and generative engines
Partner with Product Marketing, Demand Generation teams, and our digital agency to apply GEO practices consistently across priority content


Creative Leadership

Lead and develop two designers, ensuring content and design operate within scalable content systems
Partner with external agencies to extend capacity while maintaining consistency and efficiency across the content pipeline


Requirements

Content Creation, Strategy, and Campaign Execution

Serve as the content strategy lead for campaigns, shaping narratives, formats, and assets required to support technical buyers
