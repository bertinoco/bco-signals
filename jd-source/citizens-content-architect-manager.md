Description

The Content Architect Manager defines how content is structured across Citizens as a system built from reusable, machine-readable entities.

Reporting to the Head of Web Experience within the Digital Content Experience Strategy team, this role sets and evolves the enterprise content model, defining how content entities such as products, rates, disclosures, offers, FAQs, pages, and modules are structured, related, governed, and delivered across web, mobile, authenticated, and AI-driven experiences.

It sits at the intersection of content, product, and technology, translating business needs into scalable models that teams apply consistently across channels and use cases.

What You'll Do

Define and evolve the enterprise content model: types, fields, relationships, and metadata
Structure content around real-world entities (for example, products, rates, disclosures) rather than around any single channel or experience
Ensure content is independent of presentation and designed for reuse across contexts
Align content models with system architecture across CMS, API, and engineering teams so content can be created, stored, and delivered consistently
Define structured data standards, including schema.org and JSON-LD where appropriate, so content is discoverable, retrievable, and reusable by search engines, answer engines, LLMs, and internal systems
Lead cross-functional decisions on model changes, tradeoffs, and exceptions across Web Strategy, Product, Technology, Risk, and Compliance
Own the governance process for content model changes, including intake, review, approval, versioning, exception handling, and retirement of outdated structures
Drive adoption by creating practical guidance that helps teams apply models correctly in real workflows
Define content structures that can be resolved through stable API contracts and consumed consistently by downstream applications
Evaluate existing content structures and legacy CMS constraints, identifying where current models should be reused, refactored, or retired
Partner with design system and engineering teams to align content models with reusable components and front-end rendering patterns

How You Work

Play a defining role in how content structure decisions are made across the organization
Lead with an informed point of view and build trust by helping partners make better decisions
Balance long-term system integrity with near-term business needs
Hold the line on scalable structures while making pragmatic tradeoffs when business needs require exceptions

What Success Looks Like

Content is structured once and reused across channels without duplication
Content model changes are governed and do not depend on one-off project decisions
Models are applied consistently across teams and business lines
New content types are introduced without breaking existing structures
Content is retrievable and usable in AI-driven experiences
Teams work faster because the structure is clear and predictable
Teams understand when to reuse existing structures versus request new ones
Content can be delivered through stable API contracts without channel-specific rework

Required Qualifications

8+ years in content architecture, content engineering, information architecture, or a related discipline
Experience defining governance processes for content model change management
Experience partnering with engineering teams on API-driven content delivery
Ability to distinguish content structure from presentation and component implementation
Hands-on experience designing structured content models (types, fields, relationships, metadata)
Experience with headless or hybrid CMS platforms and content APIs
Strong understanding of content modeling principles, taxonomy design, and entity-based content structures
Experience operating in complex, cross-functional environments where content, product, technology, and compliance priorities intersect
Proven ability to drive decisions and alignment without direct authority
Ability to explain complex structural concepts clearly in plain language
Bachelor's degree in a relevant field, or equivalent professional experience

Preferred Qualifications

Experience designing content for AI-driven discovery (search, answer engines, LLMs)
Familiarity with schema.org, JSON-LD, and structured data validation
Experience building or contributing to content governance models
Experience in a regulated industry
Experience with design systems and component-based front-end architectures
Experience migrating or refactoring content models from legacy CMS platforms
Experience with regulated content, disclosures, consent, or approval workflows

Tools & Platforms

Headless or hybrid CMS platforms
Content APIs (REST, GraphQL) and delivery models
Taxonomy and metadata management tools
Structured data tools (schema.org, JSON-LD)
Multi-channel delivery and orchestration systems
