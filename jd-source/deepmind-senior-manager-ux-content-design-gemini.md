About the job

We’re hiring a Senior Manager to lead the content design team. This role requires high taste, creativity, and the ability to uncover top user needs and pain points. The role also requires excellent writing skills and high judgement in order to define what good quality looks like.

As the Content Design lead, you’ll play a critical role in building new experiences for the Gemini app that are both helpful and delightful for global Gemini users. You'll operate as a strategic partner across the Gemini team, collaborating cross-functionally with product designers, product managers, engineers, UX researchers, data scientists, and marketers. Together, you'll ideate, design, and implement solutions that not only enhance the user journey but also drive key growth metrics.

A core aspect of this role involves becoming an expert in our users' needs. This means gaining a profound understanding of various user behaviors, preferences, and pain points across various geographies, platforms (web, mobile, etc.), and audience segments. Responsibilities

Manage and coach a team of AI-native content designers. It will also require blending creative problem-solving, excellent writing craft, strong leadership, and a proven ability to translate user needs into design solutions that bring clarity and delight to Gemini's global user base.
Bring clarity to ambiguous product problems and shape them into innovative experiences that drive feature discovery and engagement within the Gemini App. 
Elevate content design craft and advocate user-centered design principles throughout the product development life-cycle. 
Evolve and execute on our goal for content design and UX writing in a multi-modal world, including leveraging AI to scale content design guidelines, systems, and frameworks.
